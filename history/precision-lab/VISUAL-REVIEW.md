# Visual review of the actual Precision Lab captures

This is an intermediate visual revision, not a claim that the requested photographic appearance has been achieved. Functional browser tests and shader compilation are not visual acceptance tests.

## Reviewed views

The assembly is captured at 1280 × 800 and 128 path samples per pixel. The turbine close-up is captured at 1280 × 800 and 64 samples per pixel. Reconstructed images and raw accumulated references are retained by the capture script. All pixels come from the application's WebGL canvas, without compositing or image generation.

## Visible changes

The front identity plate is physically part of the scene, inclined and supported, instead of a small horizontal patch. The shared chassis and module bases have thinner material layers, service collars, seams, fasteners and cables. The turbine exposes a bearing race, winding supports, curved blades and a drive assembly. Machined metal has a directional highlight response rather than the same uniform glossy lobe as the coated housings.

The rebuilt geological exhibit has a continuous fracture-plane silhouette rather than the rejected stacked-disk prototype. The tree has additional twig structure, varied leaves, soil and surface litter. Visibility, indirect-light caches and local reflection captures were regenerated for the new geometry. These changes do not make the natural exhibits photographic by themselves.

## Remaining visual failures

* The stone silhouette still feels overly designed and the surface relief is too uniform in some regions.
* The tree's trunk, branching distribution and repeated foliage clusters still reveal its procedural construction.
* The composited flame is the weakest natural exhibit: it does not yet have the structure, radiative interaction and subtle transparency of a convincing photographed flame.
* Some rings, grooves and high-contrast metal edges retain sampling grain or aliasing. Guided reconstruction can soften fine texture and lettering; the raw image and an off switch are included.
* The whole scene remains a designed exhibition assembly, with less variation in surface condition and construction than a photographed real instrument.

The mechanical close-up is a more convincing result than the complete assembly. The full portfolio does not yet meet a strict photographic-realism acceptance bar. Studio is progressive still-view refinement; this revision does not establish 60-fps mobile animation.
